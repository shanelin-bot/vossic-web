## The invisible half of your data center is slowing your entire business

IT has its tools. OT has its tools. Security, compliance, and access control each have their own. When something breaks, engineers jump between 3–6 platforms and piece together the answer from experience and spreadsheets. CortexDC was built to end that.

---

## Graph analytics: one graph, three analyses at once

CortexDC's flagship capability. Powered by the Apache AGE graph database, it consolidates racks, services, power paths, networks, and contracts into a single queryable topology — supporting three simultaneous analysis modes:

**Impact Analysis**
If one device goes down, which business services are affected? Traces downstream dependencies in seconds to calculate blast radius.

**Correlation Analysis**
Maps service / asset / rack / power dependencies and shared risk points. Informs redundancy planning and maintenance strategies.

**Root Cause Analysis**
Backtracks from an alert through the event chain to identify the source node. Automatically generates incident reports.
